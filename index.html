Thornroad

A gamebook of the Hush and the Cinder Spire

How this works

Thornroad is a dice-driven gamebook. You read a scene and choose what to do; the road, the dice, and your gear decide the rest.

  • Skill, Stamina, Charm — rolled once at the start. Stamina is your health: at zero, your journey ends. Skill and Charm can rise or fall in play but never above where you began.
  • Luck — six boxes. Testing your luck crosses one out; if it was already crossed, fortune wasn't with you. Crossed boxes can sometimes be cleared again.
  • Combat — each round you and a foe both roll; whoever rolls higher wounds the other for 2 Stamina (ties are a parry). Some foes have Loyalty: wound them enough, or outlast them, and they'll break and run.
  • Spells — chosen before you leave, ten charges to spend however you like across eight spells. Once cast, a spell is gone for the rest of the journey.
  • The pack — seven slots for whatever you find or buy. Choose carefully.

If your journey ends, the road remembers a little of what you learned — riddle answers and shortcuts stay with you next time.
